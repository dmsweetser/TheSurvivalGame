# Devlog — v1.5 (Nov 14, 2026)

Title: A CASTLE IN THE ARCADE

v1.5 is here, and the Arcade has a fifth cabinet.

CASTLE — a tribute to *Castle Adventure*, the 1984 DOS classic, built for a certain player who never stopped loving it. Twenty rooms of stone: 13 treasures to pocket, 15 monsters to dodge or duel, tonics waiting in the Kitchen and the Cellar, and a sword in the Armory — and take the hint the original always gave: bump into a monster without that sword and all you do is bump. The gate key sleeps in the Dungeon, under an ogre. Find the sword. Find the key. Then stroll out the Courtyard gate like you own the place. A perfect escape scores 1550 — same as 1984.

Also in 1.5:

- The title screen's header got trimmed down, so the whole menu breathes and the records at the bottom aren't crowded any more.
- Heavy hats have a floor now. However tall your carcass tower gets, your crawl never drops below a dignified trudge — rather slow, never *extra painfully slow*. And the WALK OF FAME is earned properly now: it only rains when your hat has you pinned at that minimum crawl. Just slow doesn't cut it — you have to be at the bottom.
- The Android icon's spider got a thicker frown. He was always grumpy; now you can tell from across the home screen.

Tested before it ships: castletest 25/25 (the full escape, key and sword and all), speedfloor 5/5, woftest 12/12, arcadetest 24/24, mallfix 28/28.

- One more cabinet rolled in after those notes: SUPER BUDDY CART. The Arcade grew a second row to hold it — three boxes on the north wall, three on a new island row. Three laps, six racers, boost pads on the straights, and grass that treats your kart like a lawn ornament. Your best finish and best time are kept per buddy, so the trophy has your name on it until you beat it.
