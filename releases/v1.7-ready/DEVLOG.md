# THE SURVIVAL GAME — Devlog
## v1.7 — MODE 7 (November 14, 2026)

SUPER BUDDY CART has a new seat, and it is directly behind your kart.

- **The race is now rendered SNES-style.** The world is still the same flat circuit it always was — but the camera sits on your tail and the ground plane stretches out ahead of you, Mode 7 fashion: striped grass bands rushing past, red-and-white rumble strips on the edges, boost pads lying flat on the tarmac, a checkered start line, a low sun, and a tree line that slides sideways as you steer. Your kart sits big at the bottom of the screen, exhaust flames flickering when you ride a boost pad, and rival karts loom larger as you run them down. A little circuit map in the corner tracks every kart on the lap.
- **HEAD-TO-HEAD, with a WAITING ROOM** (the headline act that landed during 1.6's run, now official): picking the cart cabinet on your own side of the screen puts you in the waiting room, where you pick your kart's colour — RED, BLUE, YELLOW, GREEN, PURPLE, ORANGE, and no two humans may share. If the other player picks the cart on *their* side too, the rooms link; the race starts when both of you ready up. One shared race, four AI rivals, records saved for both buddies. Leave mid-race and your kart becomes an AI drone; if the host leaves, the race ends for both.
- Same 3 laps, same five smug rivals, same rubber-banding. The trophy is still extremely smug.

Tested, as ever, until the suites begged for mercy (carttest 22/22, cartcoop 24/24, and the whole arcade still standing).

The waiting room is open. Pick a colour.
