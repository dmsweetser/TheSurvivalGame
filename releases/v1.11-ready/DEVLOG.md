# THE SURVIVAL GAME — Devlog
## v1.11 — OUT FROM THE EPICENTER (November 14, 2026)

The main world has always been eight regions radiating out from the epicenter — Clean Code Meadows up close, the Unmaintained Depths way out where the bugs get big. As of today, the Arcade knows that map too. Super Buddy Run and Super Buddy Cart run on the same eight regions, in the same order, wearing the same colours, crawling with the same strains. Learn one game and you've learned the geography of all of them.

**SUPER BUDDY RUN:**

- **Every level is a region now.** Level 1 is the Meadows, level 5 is the Frozen Cache Fields, level 6 is the Neon RAM Flats at dusk under a pale moon, level 8 is the Depths — and then it wraps around, harder. Skies, ground, and the dominant bug strain all match the main game, and the bugs get quicker the further out you run.
- **Stomp chains.** Bounce from bug to bug without touching the ground and the points double each hop: 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600. The ground is lava. The ground was always lava.
- **There's a bonus vault.** The tallest pipe in each level wears a gold band. Stand on it and press DOWN: a quiet room full of bits and a spare heart, no bugs invited. Down the exit pipe and you're back on the run exactly where you left it.

**SUPER BUDDY CART — now a Grand Prix:**

- **Eight circuits, one per region**, from the friendly Meadows oval to shapes out in the Depths that have no business calling themselves ovals. Each track wears its region's colours, and the gantry flies its name.
- **Two cups of four races each**: the HOME CUP (Meadows through Kernel Badlands) and the FAR CUP (Cache Fields through the Depths). The host picks the cup in the waiting room with up/down; in co-op, both players race the same cup.
- **Points make the champion**: 10/8/6/5/4/3 by finish place, standings on screen between races, and a final rank at the end that your buddy keeps — a separate best for each cup. Winning one race is nice. Winning the cup is the job.

**BUDDY CRAFT:**

- **Search.** The list outgrew scrolling, especially on a phone. Press `/` (or tap the search row) and type — the list narrows to what matches as you go. ESC clears it and brings the whole list back.

**CASTLE:**

- **No key, no leaving.** The door doesn't care how sorry you are. Somewhere in those twenty rooms is a key, and until it's in your pocket the only way out is the way you wouldn't choose. (House rule, straight from the top.)

**THE BUG MALL:**

- The Walk of Fame got **more confetti**. Substantially more. It was felt that the previous amount did not do justice to a buddy crawling at minimum speed under a hat the size of a house.
