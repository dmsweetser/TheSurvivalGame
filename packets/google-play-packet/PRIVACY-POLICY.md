# Privacy Policy — The Survival Game

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

The Survival Game is a fully offline game. It does not collect,
store, transmit, or share any personal data — or any data at all.

- The app requests **no permissions**.
- It contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
  SDKs** that collect data.
- It has **no accounts and no sign-in**.
- Your game progress is saved only on your own device, and is
  never sent anywhere.
- The app makes **no network requests** to collect information
  about you.

If this policy ever changes, the updated policy will be posted at
this address before any change takes effect.
