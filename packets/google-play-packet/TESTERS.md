Rules that keep the run valid:
- Opted in ≠ invited. A tester counts only after they accept the
  opt-in link AND install from the closed track on a real device.
- Anyone who opts out restarts their own 14 days. Check this table
  every few days during the run.
- Collect at least a line of feedback per tester — the production
  application questionnaire asks what feedback came in and what
  changed because of it.
