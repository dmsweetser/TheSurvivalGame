## About this game (long description)

> **The bugs are out to get you. Fortunately, they're also currency.**
>
> THE SURVIVAL GAME is a cartoony top-down survival shooter in the
> spirit of classic arena survival — except the horde is made of
> software bugs, your arsenal is office equipment, and every kill
> sticks to your head.
>
> **Your hat is your wallet.** Every bug you squash becomes a carcass
> on the stack on your head, growing through glorious tiers from BUG
> CAP to STACK OVERFLOW to THE LEGACY SYSTEM. A heavy hat is a rich
> hat — and a slow one. Haul it back to the Bug Bounty store at the
> Epicenter and pay by weight (watch the carcasses get pulled off your
> head at the till) for better weapons, shields, armor and espresso.
> Or spend three carcasses to throw down a carcass wall the swarm has
> to chew through.
>
> **The further out, the bigger they get.** Near the Epicenter the
> bugs are few, tiny and slow — pocket change. Real money lives far
> from home: giants worth over 100 kg, dense swarms, and little bugs
> that suddenly run very fast. The Epicenter itself is NOT a safe
> zone; they will follow you right up to the store counter.
>
> **Keyboard only, Cyberdogs-style.** No mouse. You face the way you
> move and you fire the way you face. Space fires. That's it — pure
> 1994 energy.
>
> **Bring friends.** Run the game and you're hosting: friends join
> from any web browser on your network — no extra installs for them.
> The run only ends when every player is down. The leaderboard tracks
> the only stat that matters — heaviest hat.
>
> **Shop safe.** The Epicenter is no safe zone — bugs wander right
> in — but while anyone is in the store, a shield seals the plaza
> and holds them at the ring.
>
> **Arsenal:** Mechanical Keyboard · Mouse Tosser · Rubber Duck
> Cannon · PM Launcher (now with SYNERGY!) · The Debugger
> **Store upgrades:** Overclock · Caffeine Drip · Firewall Shield ·
> Hoodie Armor · Espresso Legs · Rollback Token · Hotfix (heals 50 HP)
>
> The world is infinite, procedural, and auto-saves as you go. How
> heavy can your hat get?

## Pricing recommendation

**$4.99**, optionally with a 10–20% launch-week discount.

- The $100 Steam Direct fee is recouped once the game reaches $1,000
  adjusted gross revenue ≈ 200 copies at $4.99.
- Steam's cut is 30%; you keep 70% up to $10M.
- Free is the other sane option if you'd rather maximize players than
  revenue — but you can't switch from paid to free and back casually,
  so decide once.

## Content / rating questionnaire notes (for the IARC form)

- Violence: cartoon/fantasy violence only — bugs squash into cartoon
  goo, no blood, no gore, no human targets.
- No sexual content, no gambling, no substances, no profanity.
- Multiplayer is LAN-only; no public servers, no voice/text chat with
  strangers, no user-generated content shared online.
- The game collects no data and has no analytics.
- Steamworks also has an AI-content disclosure section — it asks about
  AI-generated content that ships in the game (art, audio, text
  players consume). All art and music here is procedural code; answer
  the form as you judge it.
