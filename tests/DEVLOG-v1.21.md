# The Survival Game v1.21 — "A FAIR FIGHT"

For eight regions, the hive fought on conjured bugs. Waves arrived free, forever, on a timer — while every buddy you fielded came out of a purse your harvesters filled one load at a time. That ends now. The hive has a wallet, and it can go broke.

## THE HIVE PAYS ITS WAY

- **Every hive keeps its own purse** — 150 bits at the first bell — and every bug it sends is bought out of it: crawlers 20, spitters 35.
- **Bug harvesters.** Each hive fields two squat workers in gold-rimmed packs who run the same loop yours do: out to a bit node, scoop, haul it home. They draw from the SAME nodes. A node the hive drains is a node you don't get.
- **Waves are bought, not conjured.** When the wave timer fires, a hive replaces its dead harvesters first — a hive that can't harvest can't fight — then buys as many bugs as the purse covers. Short purse, short wave. Empty purse, silence.
- **Guards cost too.** The free defender drip is gone; a hive refills its guard post only out of real surplus.
- **You can watch it breathe.** A scouted hive prints its purse right under its name. Starve it and watch the number fall.
- **Kill the economy, kill the war.** Snipe their harvesters and the waves thin out, then stop altogether. From here on, battles are decided by skill and strategy — not by which side gets free bugs.

## DEMOLISH

- **Sell any building back for half its build cost** — Patch Bay 75, Assembly 100, Zapper 50, Firewall 12, Gate 17. It's in the building's own command bar, gamepad capture and all: one tap arms it, the second confirms, and B backs out safely.
- The Compiler Core, obviously, is not for sale.

## A NEW COAT OF WAR

- **The field got dressed.** Survey-grid ground with real grain, bit nodes that glow, pulse — and visibly shrink and dim as they drain, so you can see both economies working.
- **Hits flash, the fallen poof**, harvests sparkle on the scoop and the deposit, and shots leave tracers.
- **Buildings got finished**: the Core wears a halo, the Zapper's charge glows hot after a shot, work lights blink on the Patch Bay and the Assembly while they build, and the hive breathes. Slowly.
- **The fog rolls in soft now** — no more hard tile edge where your vision ends.
- And the **PATCHER has been re-issued**: olive patrol helmet with the red cross, medkit satchel on its back. Same 45 bits, same tireless legs, new uniform. It's a field medic now.

Commanders: the swarm is on a budget. Make it regret every bit.
