# v1.22 — THE GLOW-UP

The whole game just got a fresh coat of paint, and two sneaky bugs got squashed flat.

**THE FIX:** CASTLE was never supposed to be a prison. Somewhere along the way the castle door started demanding the gate key before it would let you walk back to the Arcade — so if you wandered in curious, you were staying until you finished the whole dungeon. That was never the deal. The exit is open now, always, key or no key: stroll in, look around, leave whenever you like. The key still guards the courtyard gate — escaping WITH the treasures is still the win. Leaving is just leaving.

**THE OTHER FIX:** WAR! players on a gamepad know the moment — you wake the command cursor, tap A on the little Zapper Tower sitting dead centre of your screen… and nothing. The cursor was waking up half a command-bar BELOW the middle of the screen, close enough to look right, far enough to miss every small building by a whisker. It wakes up dead centre now, the way it always should have. Wake it, tap A, build the thing.

**THE SHINE:**
- The castle is LIT. Every doorway in CASTLE wears a pair of flickering torches, the flagstones finally have grit and shade of their own, treasures breathe a slow golden halo across the room, and the snakes, ogres and demons cast proper shadows. (The bat still doesn't. It flies. It's smug about it.)
- The title logo stands up off the screen with a real drop shadow, and the save-slot cards and the game-over panel wear the same gold-cornered chrome as the rest of the HUD.
- The zone readout up top matches the status card and the leaderboard now — one family of panels, no odd one out.
- The Bug Mall's hanging lanterns actually cast light.
- SUPER BUDDY RUN's crawlers, shells, shrooms and flowers sit on the ground with shadows under them instead of hovering on good intentions.
- TANK RESCUE explosions flash hot inside their shockwave rings, the way explosions frankly should.

Nothing moved. Nothing costs more, hits harder, or behaves differently — every price, every tap target, every save file is exactly where you left it. It's the same game, wearing its Sunday best.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
