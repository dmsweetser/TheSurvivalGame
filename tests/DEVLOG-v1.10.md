# THE SURVIVAL GAME — Devlog
## v1.10 — FIRE IS GO (November 14, 2026)

The kart game changed how it drives, and got a makeover while it was at it. (House rule from today: a change this size gets its own version — so here it is, 1.10.)

**SUPER BUDDY CART:**

- **New way to drive: hold FIRE to go.** The shoot button is the gas now — the stick (or the arrows) is purely for steering. No more holding up until your thumb files a complaint. Down still brakes and reverses; let go of FIRE and you coast. Both players, same deal: player two holds pad A.
- **The circuit is prettier**: a gradient sky with drifting clouds and a haloed sun, a far hill line behind the trees, blossom trees in the infield, a manicured apron around the asphalt, and crisp white edge lines inside the rumble strips.
- **The karts got shaded** — sunlit tops, dark skirts, drivers bobbing at speed — and working engines now puff little exhaust clouds. Boost pads pulse and glow.
- **Race day dressing**: flags waving on the start gantry, a red-and-white awning on the grandstand, and confetti over the finish.
- **The race talks to you**: gain a place and it says so ("UP TO P1!"), and the final lap gets its own shout.

Everything from 1.9 rides along: your Banzai Buddy in your kart, the buddy rivals, the drift, the gentler Tank Rescue with its grenades.
