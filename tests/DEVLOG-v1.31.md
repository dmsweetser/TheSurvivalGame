# v1.31 — EVERY BUTTON, TOO

v1.30 walked every button in the game and wrote down nine promises it couldn't keep on the day. This release keeps them. No new cabinets, no new modes — just the nine loose ends, tied.

**THE CANNON IS BACK:** Last release, Tank Rescue on the phone's DRAG scheme had no cannon button at all, and all we could do was make the welcome note stop lying about it. The decision took a day, not forever: the Tank deck is now drawn on DRAG — a big honest FIRE button, and the weapon switch above it wearing its proper name, WALL-side and all. Hold FIRE, the cannon fires. Tap the switch, grenades. Tank crews on DRAG are full crews again.

**THE BEAN, EXPLAINED:** The Backfire Bean is the fastest way home in the game, and HOW TO PLAY never mentioned it — on any device. It does now, in your device's own words: Y on a pad, the bean readout tap on a phone, 6 on a keyboard. Squeeze him, PBBBT, home in a flash. The same page also finally explains weapon switching on a phone — the WPN button and the weapon chips, both schemes.

**ONE NAME PER BUTTON:** The touch deck in Banzai World captioned its buttons one way (PICK! THROW! PATCH!) while the prompts at the bottom named them another (FIRE, USE, WALL). Now the deck wears the prompt names everywhere — FIRE, USE, WALL — so the button you're told to press is the button you can see.

**SETTINGS, COMPLETE:** The Settings screen's gamepad summary stopped halfway — it never mentioned the bean, weapon switching, sprint, or how to leave. The summary is finished, and the screen itself grew a BACK row at the bottom that a touch player can tap. Settings is no longer a room with no door.

**THE MOUSE MOVES IN:** The Windows, Linux and Mac builds took keyboard and gamepad and ignored the mouse entirely — every tap-driven panel in them was a picture, not a control. The mouse now rides the same road it does on the web: a click is a tap, a drag is a drag, everywhere the web build answers one.

**A CHIP FOR THE HOME GAME:** Banzai World has had a tappable START chip for pausing; the main game never got one, so a touch player out in the meadow had no way to pause at all. The chip is home now — top of the screen, under the zone banner, same gold bars.

**WAR! PAUSE, BY ARROW:** The battle pause in WAR! took keys 1, 2 and 3 and nothing else — arrow keys walked in and did nothing. Arrows walk the choices now, Enter picks, the highlight shows where you stand, and 1/2/3 still work for the veterans.

**CRAFT SEARCH, BY PAD:** Buddy Craft's search wanted a "/" key or a fingertip; a gamepad had neither. Pad X starts the search now, and the screen says so.

Nothing moved. Nothing got harder or easier. Nine promises, nine kept.
