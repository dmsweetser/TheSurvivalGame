# v1.36 — DOME MONEY

Crispy Dirt pays better, shops earlier, and has learned to duck.

**STARTING CASH: 100 BITS A SIDE.** The old 50-bit purse bought one Big Carcass and an apology. A hundred buys options: a Honey Pot and a Dirt Clod, or a Firewall with enough left over for tracers, or the discipline to save toward a Cluster. The Backfire Bean still costs 150, because some prizes stay earned. The battery gets the same hundred, and it has been spending it. You have been warned.

**THE FIREWALL.** Eighty bits raises a dome of firewall sparks over your gunner — the same blue language the main game's shield has always spoken. Blasts hit the dome first: strength 100, eaten point for point, and only what overflows ever touches the catapult underneath. It holds against honey, too. What it does not hold against is the ground leaving — falls bypass it completely, because a dome stops explosions, not gravity. The shop line says so in plain letters: BLOCKS BLASTS, NOT FALLS. The strength rides across rounds, worn down to whatever the last round left of it, and buying again is a recharge back to the full hundred — no partial top-ups, no haggling. The number is always printed over the dome, so you never have to guess how much sky you have left. The bugs buy it as well: on HARD it is the first thing on their list, and a shielded spitter is exactly as smug as you imagine.

**THE SHOP OPENS BEFORE ROUND 1.** No more walking into the first hills with stock parts. Difficulty, then the Field Shop, then the dirt — and in hot-seat 2P both gunners stock up in turn, each from their own hundred, before a single carcass flies. Buying nothing remains a proud and legal tradition: take the TO BATTLE row and start clean.

**THE SOURCE ROOM, IN LARGER PRINT.** Somewhere in the Bug Mall there is a room whose terminal has been squinting at its own source code since it opened. The font has gone up a full step — every list, editor, prompt and footer reflowed to the new size, nothing clipped, nothing spilling. The code was always beautiful. Now it is beautiful at reading distance.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
