# v1.15 — OPEN GROUND

WAR! loosens its grip and hardens its fists.

**YOUR LAND, ONLY BIGGER.** Building in WAR! used to demand that a new
structure sit square inside the territory your base claims. No longer.
Your land now reaches further from every building you own — and a new
build only has to *touch* it. Lean a zapper tower out past the old
line, stretch your firewalls segment by stubborn segment: if it
touches home, it stands.

**BREAK EVERYTHING.** The trees, rocks, cable spools, broken terminals
and rubble heaps scattered over every battlefield are no longer
scenery — they're obstacles, and obstacles have hit points. Select
your fighters, tap the offending tree, and watch it come down. Rubble
crumbles fast; rocks are stubborn (bring friends, or a tank — though
the Siege Tank's double damage is reserved for structures, and a rock
is not a structure). Cleared ground stays cleared for the rest of the
battle, so think before you clear: that tree line was cover for
somebody.

**A SMALLER MERCY.** Selected buddies fighting in the fog now show
their faces again, instead of hiding under a solid green blob.
Commanders deserve to see who's fighting for them.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
