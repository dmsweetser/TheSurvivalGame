# v1.24 — POLISHED SEAMS

No new countries this time. No new cabinets. This build went back over the whole game with a cloth and a wrench, and everything it touched either shines more or works the way it always should have.

**THE WRENCH:** Holding BACKSPACE in the Whales Only code box now chews through the code the way holding it everywhere else does — one press deletes one letter, a good long hold deletes the rest, exactly as designed. (The web shell was swallowing the held key before the game ever felt it; the native apps never had the problem. They have been told to stop showing off.)

**THE CLOTH:** Walking through a doorway used to be a hard cut — now the world blinks. A soft dark veil lifts whenever you step between the menu and the meadow, into the Mall, out to the Arcade, or through a Banzai World gate, quick as an eyelid. Split-screen co-op grew proper chrome: the seam between the two views is a gold-hearted groove now, with a little P1 and P2 chip in each player's own hoodie colour riding it, so nobody has to ask which half is theirs. The Walk of Fame marquee got its bulbs — a chasing row of confetti-coloured lamps over the parade, lit while you crawl the Mall under a hat too heavy for dignity. The Settings and How To Play pages sit in the same gold-cornered panels as the rest of the game's furniture. Bug Bowl's lane no longer floats in a black void: there's a speckled alley wall behind it, warm light pooled on the pin deck, gutters with actual shadows, and a wooden approach under the bowler's feet. The meadow air itself is alive now — motes drift in the daylight, bobbing over the grass. And if you've flipped on EDDIE MODE, the puffball treatment now reaches Super Buddy Run: the crawlers out there are fuzzy, orange, and extremely nosey, just like their cousins back home.

Same bugs. Same hats. Smoother seams.
