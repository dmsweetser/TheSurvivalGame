# v1.28 — THERE AND BACK AGAIN

The Arcade has been keeping a space at the end of the row. Eight cabinets stand there now, and the eighth one is not a game you finish in an afternoon. It is a road.

**MORDOR FUN RUN:** A new cabinet at the west end of the WAR! row. Your buddy at the Shire, the Black Gate at the far end of the world, and between them a road that wanders the way roads do — out of the green hills, through the deep forest, across the golden plains, into the rocky wastes, and at last onto the ash. The whole way is measured the way it should be: in MOOSE. One moose is 460 units of road, the route runs a thousand of them, and there is a real moose standing at the start line next to a measured track so you can check our arithmetic. Moose markers stand every fifty moose along the way, and every one you pass is banked — in gold, in the save, for good.

**THE ROAD IS NOT EMPTY:** Goblins from the first steps. Wolves once the trees thicken. Spiders in the dark country. Orcs standing guard over the last half of the world, and worse tempers the closer you get to the fire. They fight in real time, with the swing your buddy already knows — the road is run on your legs and won with your arms, and training done elsewhere pays off here, because a stronger buddy is a stronger runner. Kills are for survival. The road pays its respects at the end.

**AND THE RUN WAITS FOR YOU:** This is the heart of it. A thousand moose is not one sitting, and the game knows it. Leave the cabinet mid-run — leave the Arcade, leave the game, close the whole thing and go to bed — and the run stays exactly where you left it: your distance, your marker, your health, your clock. Come back and the cabinet asks, plain as day: RESUME, NEW RUN, or LEAVE. Resume puts you on the very spot you stopped. New Run asks twice before it scraps anything. And if the road knocks you down, you wake up hale at the last banked marker, the stretch ahead reset, everything already banked still banked. The road is patient. It can afford to be. It's a thousand moose long.

**THE TROPHY CASE:** Finish the run — actually stand at the Black Gate — and the Trophy Case gains a MORDOR FUN RUN row with your best time on it, and the run pays out experience like it means it. One standing rule holds, as it holds everywhere: ZEN MODE sets no records. A zen stroll to Mordor is its own reward.
