# v1.13 — WAR!

The Arcade has a seventh cabinet, and it is not like the others. WAR! is a real-time strategy game: you, a Compiler Core, a handful of Banzai Buddies, and the entire bug swarm between you and the far edge of the map.

**The campaign is the world itself.** WAR! opens on the conquest map — all eight regions ringed around the epicenter, the same Meadows-to-Depths geography as the main game, the Run, and the Cart. Clean Code Meadows is waiting. The Unmaintained Depths are locked, and they can stay that way until you've earned them. Win a region and it's yours for good — the conquest is saved on your buddy, and the next region unlocks.

**Every battle is an economy with teeth.** Harvesters haul BITS from the gold code nodes back to your Core. Bits train buddies at the Core and the Patch Bay: wrench-swinging melee fighters who wade in, and ranged buddies who pick bugs off from a distance. Bits build ZAPPER TOWERS and FIREWALLS to hold the line when the hive sends a wave — and it will send waves. Spend too slowly and you're overrun; spend too fast and there's nothing left when it matters. Destroy every hive on the map and the region falls. Lose the Core and you march home and try again.

**Built for thumbs.** Tap a buddy to select him. Double-tap to select every buddy of his kind on screen. Tap the ground to move, tap a bug to attack it, tap your buildings for their command bar, drag to pan the battlefield. A mouse does all of the same tricks on desktop — clicks are taps now, everywhere in the game.

Two honest notes: WAR! is a solo command for now (your co-op partner gets his own war, not a shared one), and the balance is a first campaign draft — later regions bite harder than earlier ones, but exactly how hard is something your playtesting gets a vote on.

The Arcade is now seven fine games. The seventh one is a war.
