# The Survival Game v1.18 — "SHIFTING GROUND"

Commanders: the ground has opinions now, and it changes them daily.

## WAR! — NO TWO BATTLES ALIKE

Every WAR! battle now rolls its own field the moment you march in:

- **The hives move.** The bug hive — or hives, in the meaner regions — nests somewhere new along the far side every single battle. North corner, deep south, dead centre: scout it through the fog like you mean it, because last battle's map is this battle's lie.
- **Barrier lines.** Runs of dark, indestructible slabs now stand across the midfield, and no two battles place them alike. Nothing breaks them — not buddies, not siege shells, not bugs. Everybody routes around. The good news: every line is rolled with a gap in it somewhere. The better news: your army's pathfinding already knows how to thread it. The honest news: so do the bugs.
- **The debris re-rolls too.** Trees, rocks and dead terminals scatter fresh each battle, so even the salvage map is a new puzzle.

Marching east on muscle memory is over. Read the field, find the gap, and fight the battle that's actually in front of you.

## SAVE & QUIT — THE MAP REMEMBERS (FIXED)

Last version's Save & Quit had a trap in it: park a battle, come back to the world map, tap your region to jump back in — and the game started a *fresh* battle over your parked one. Commanders lost campaigns to that tap. Our war-room drills only ever re-entered through the arcade cabinet door, so the trap survived every test. A commander's field report caught it. Thank you.

Now the map itself keeps the faith: the region holding your parked battle wears an **IN PROGRESS** marker, tapping it resumes the fight exactly where you left it, and no fresh battle anywhere can quietly overwrite the one you parked.
