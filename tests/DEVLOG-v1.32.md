# v1.32 "SPIT-SHINE"

The pause button has moved out of the hat stack's personal space, a party
crash ends, and every surface in the game gets a wash, a comb, and a hard
look in the mirror.

**THE PAUSE CHIP MOVES HOUSE.** On touch, the main game's pause chip used
to float mid-screen, parked directly on top of your own hat — the one
place guaranteed to be full of bugs. It now lives in the top-right corner,
tucked under the leaderboard where a pause button belongs. Same chip, same
tap, better neighbourhood.

**CONFETTI SQUASH PAYS ITS OWN WAY AGAIN.** The party burst on a squash
is a Whales Only luxury, and somewhere along the road it started firing
for players who had never bought it — a glitch in the save ledger let a
switched-on flag survive without the purchase behind it. The ledger now
balances itself at every door: if you didn't buy it, it doesn't burst.
Owners: nothing changes. Your confetti is exactly where you left it.

**THE MORDOR CABINET CAN BE READ AGAIN.** The eighth cabinet's marquee
was black on black — its colour had been missing from the paintbox
since the cabinet arrived, so the name never stood a chance. It wears
pale ash now, Mordor's own colour, with the mountain and its glowing
crack on the screen where another game's picture used to hang.

**COSMETIC PASS No. 3 — WITH ENHANCEMENTS.** Every major surface
photographed, desktop and phone, the nits evicted, and then a fresh
coat of life over the whole game: scrub and pebbles on the Tank Rescue
field, carnival bulbs on the Cart start banner, a string of party
lights along the Bug Mall's north wall and dust in its warm air, stars
twinkling over the WAR! map and over the dark countries in Super Buddy
Run, a breathing epicentre, carpet flecks in the Arcade, pebbles worn
into the Mordor road, halos behind the big menu titles, and green dust
drifting in the Source Room's light.

The nits, evicted:

- The pause menu's SKIP FIRST SHIFT row no longer strikes through the
  "YOUR HAT IS SAFE HERE" line.
- The game-over report prints its whole first line — no more
  "SURVIVED 0:01 - SQUASHED 0 - CAS.." cliffhanger.
- The WAR! map's BADLANDS label no longer hides under the SKIRMISH
  button.
- On the Super Buddy Cart grid, the track's name no longer collides
  with the lap counter in the top band.
- In Entrances, the buddy card steps aside for the START chip — the
  whole name fits again, HP bar and all.
- Castle's banner and HP bar get the same courtesy.

No balance, difficulty, economy or content changes. Saves carry over.
