# The Survival Game v1.19 — "BOOT CAMP"

Your buddy has been watching you win from the sidelines for six versions. No more. Everybody trains now.

## THE ARCADE PAYS YOUR BUDDY

Every cabinet success now puts XP in your Banzai Buddy's pocket — real XP, the kind that buys training and taught moves at the buddy store:

- **SUPER BUDDY RUN:** finish a level, +25 XP.
- **SUPER BUDDY CART:** +40 for the win, +30 / +20 for the podium, +10 just for finishing — and a Grand Prix crown pays an extra +40.
- **CASTLE:** escape with the key, +50 XP.
- **TANK RESCUE:** mission complete, +40 XP.
- **BUDDY CRAFT:** every finished craft, +8 XP. Cook, bake, build — it all counts.
- **ENTRANCES:** make it out, +20 XP on top of the quest pay.
- **WAR!:** conquer a region for the first time, +60 XP and +10 more per region deep. Win a replay, +25. And skirmishes pay too…

## WAR! SKIRMISH — WAR WITHOUT THE WAR

New button on the world map: **SKIRMISH**. Pick a field — **SMALL** (a tight 1200×900), **STANDARD**, or **LARGE** (the standard field, packed: thirteen nodes, four barrier lines, a 400-bit purse) — pick an enemy — **EASY** (one hive, gentle bugs), **NORMAL**, or **HARD** (three hives, region-six teeth) — and fight. That's it. No campaign strings: your conquests don't move, nothing unlocks, and a skirmish is never saved over your parked campaign battle. Win one and your buddy banks **+30 / +50 / +80 XP** by difficulty. It's the training ground the campaign never had.

## RICHER FIELDS, BREATHING ROOM, AND A CREW

- **The money struggle is over.** Campaign fields now carry **eleven bit nodes worth 8,200 bits** (was nine and 4,800), harvesters carry **30** a trip, and you open with **300 bits**.
- **The first wave waits.** Fresh battles — campaign and skirmish alike — hold their first wave for at least 45 seconds. Build your economy before the sky falls.
- **Meet the PATCHER.** The Patch Bay now trains a repair buddy (45 bits, white cap, red cross, no weapon). It **autoseeks**: left alone, it finds your most hurt building, walks over, and fixes it — 12 HP a second at a bit per 4 HP — then rolls to the next one. Send it somewhere yourself and it obeys first, then goes back to work. Buildings stop dying while you weren't looking.
- **Tap a selected building again to unselect it.** No more tapping a different building just to clear the one you're on.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
