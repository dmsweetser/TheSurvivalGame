# The Survival Game v1.20 — "TAKING COMMAND"

A WAR! commander with a gamepad had a problem: tap a building with the cursor, and its orders sat in the command bar at the bottom of the screen — a place the cursor could not go. You could select anything and command nothing. Fixed, the way it should have worked from the start:

## THE BAR TAKES THE CURSOR

- **Select a building on the pad and the command bar captures the cursor.** The highlight lands right on the building's options — no flying down to the menu, no hunting. The menu is simply where you are now.
- **Navigate inside the menu only.** Stick or bumpers walk the options; with a category open, push down to step to the category row and switch, up to come back. **A** gives the order.
- **Training keeps you in the menu.** Queue a Patcher, queue three more — the bar holds you until you're done.
- **Placing a building hands the cursor back.** The moment you pick something to build, you're back on the field with the cursor under your thumb, ready to put it down.
- **B backs out, one layer at a time.** Out of the open category first, then out of the selection altogether — cancel means cancel.
- Touch and mouse command exactly as before. Nothing about them changed.

Commanders: your Patch Bay is open for business again.
