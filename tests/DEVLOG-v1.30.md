# v1.30 — EVERY BUTTON

No new cabinets. No new modes. This release has one job: every button, stick, tap and drag in the game was walked end to end — the meadow, the Mall, the Arcade, every cabinet, the Source Room — on keyboard, on gamepad, on both phone schemes, and by mouse. Whatever lied, stuck, or pointed at the wrong button got fixed.

**THE BIG ONE:** On a phone, the default way to play is DRAG — and a DRAG player who walked into the Bug Mall could not open a single shop, ride a single cabinet, or leave. The door in worked; nothing inside did, and the only way out was quitting. A tap inside the Mall now means what it means everywhere else in the game: interact. Doors, counters, the exit mat — all of them answer a tap, the same as they always did out in Banzai World. Mouse clicks on a desktop ride the same road and work the same way.

**THE LANES ARE OPEN:** Bug Bowl had the same phone trouble — the lanes swallowed every tap whole, so touch players could not aim, throw, or start a game. Now a tap throws, a held finger drags the thrower along the lane, and the prompt at the bottom names the inputs you're actually holding. The Cart waiting room takes a tap as "ready" too, and the Run's level select confirms on a tap instead of hiding the gesture.

**MENUS THAT POINT THE RIGHT WAY:** Cabinet panels used to advance no matter which way you pressed — Up and Down both walked forward. Up walks up now, everywhere, for both players. The Settings key-capture can be cancelled from a gamepad or a tap instead of trapping anyone who wandered in without a keyboard. And the prompts learned manners across the whole game: the Whales Only till honours the "A - REDEEM" it always promised pad players (and a tap outside cancels a code now), the Source Room's pad footer no longer sends B to a build it can't do, CASTLE's CONTROLS panel is Castle's own instead of Tank Rescue's hand-me-downs, and a long list of footers, blurbs and headers now name the button on the device in your hands — including your own rebound keys, not just the defaults.

**SMALL MERCY FOR TANK CREWS:** Tank Rescue on the DRAG scheme never had a cannon button at all — but its welcome note cheerfully claimed a tap fires it. The note now tells the truth and points at the scheme that works. The cannon itself is a decision for another day; the lying is over today.

**READABILITY:** The Whales list no longer prints COMICALLY ENORMOUS GLASSES through its own price tag, the shop blurb stays inside its box, and the Banzai START chip no longer sits on top of the Mordor road's name (or the Run, Craft and Tank readouts that shared its corner).

Nothing moved. Nothing got harder or easier. The game just answers every hand the way it always claimed to.
