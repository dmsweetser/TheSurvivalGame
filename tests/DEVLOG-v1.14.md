# v1.14 — WAR MACHINES

WAR! got its first field report from the front — from Daniel, naturally — and every note in it made the game better. The war effort escalates:

**You can only build on your own land.** Every structure you own now projects a property line around itself, shown as a glowing zone while you place. Build inside it and your territory grows; reach too far and the order comes back refused: TOO FAR FROM YOUR BASE. Even firewall segments extend the line, so a wall can march outward one segment at a time.

**And those firewalls finally behave like walls.** Placement snaps to a grid and every segment joins its neighbours — horizontal runs, vertical runs, corners — one continuous orange barrier instead of a row of lonely planks.

**The Assembly Plant is open.** A new 200-bit factory that rolls out two vehicles: the SCOUT BUGGY, fast and fragile with the widest eyes on the field, and the SIEGE TANK, slow, armoured, and hitting buildings — hives above all — at double damage. Selection grows up too: one tap picks a unit, two taps pick its kind, and now THREE taps muster every fighting unit you own.

**The map is dark, and it stays learned.** Fog of war covers every battlefield: black where you've never been, dim where you've explored, clear where your forces can see. Explored ground never re-fogs. Enemy units hide in the dark; hives, terrain and bit nodes stay on the map once discovered. Send the Scout out — that's what it's for. The fields themselves are dressed now, too: trees in every region's colours, rocks, broken terminals and cable spools to fight around.

**The camera learned manners.** Dragging now grabs the world itself — the spot under your finger stays under your finger, the way a map should. No more chasing the battlefield across the screen.
