# v1.35 — CRISPY DIRT

A ninth cabinet rolls into the Arcade, and it brought its own weather.

**CRISPY DIRT.** At the east end of the Arcade's top row stands a cabinet that smells faintly of turned earth and gunpowder's older cousin. Your buddy works a catapult on one dirt hill. Across a valley — always a valley, the hills insist — a battery of bugs has dug itself in, and it has opinions. This is artillery the old way: pick an angle, pick a power, read the wind, and lob a carcass in a high, hopeful arc. The dirt is fully destructible, which cuts both ways: every crater you carve stays carved, every gun can be undermined, and a unit whose hill vanishes out from under it takes the fall it has coming. Best of three rounds takes the match.

**THE WIND IS A LIAR WITH A NUMBER ON IT.** Every turn re-rolls the wind, −30 to +30, printed right there on the HUD before you commit. Ignore it and your beautiful 45-degree solution lands in the next county. Respect it and you too can drop a carcass down a spitter's snout from clear across the field.

**THE ARSENAL.** The plain Carcass is free forever. Everything else is bought with bits — and bits are strictly session money here: +10 for every enemy a blast hurts, +50 for a kill, +100 for a round, spent between rounds at the Field Shop and gone when you walk away. Tracer Carcasses (5) scout the arc for free of consequence. Big Carcasses (40) move serious dirt. Dirt Clods (30) ADD dirt — build a wall, fill a grave, bury a problem. Honey Pots (70) leave a burning pool that keeps billing the tenant every turn. Cluster Carcasses (90) break into three at the top of the arc. And the Backfire Bean (150) rolls its own yield the moment it leaves the arm — sometimes a crater you could park in, sometimes a dud with the blast radius of a sneeze. The bugs shop too. Big Carcasses first. They learn.

**THE LADDER.** Solo comes in EASY (one bug, generous aim), NORMAL (two bugs, steadier), and HARD (three bugs who barely miss), paying 30 / 50 / 80 Buddy XP once, at the match's end, for a win. Win on HARD and the Trophy Case gets a new plaque: CRISPY DIRT CHAMPION. Or pass the controls back and forth instead — 2P HOT-SEAT puts a second buddy on the far catapult, same screen, best of three, glory only. No XP changes hands at a shared screen. Those are the rules and the rules are the rules.

Every control scheme drives it: arrows and Space, a pad, a mouse that clicks and drags the gauges like it means it, and touch — drag the ANGLE and POWER bars, tap a weapon chip, and rest your finger on the big red FIRE until you're sure. It fires once, when you press, like a button should.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
