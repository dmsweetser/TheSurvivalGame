# v1.12 — STEADY FEET

A feel-and-fairness pass, straight from the playtest couch.

**SUPER BUDDY RUN slows down.** The little guy was a greased marble: full sprint almost immediately, and letting go of the stick meant sliding through two more screens out of sheer momentum. Top speed is down about a quarter, the brakes actually brake now, and a stop is a stop. The jump is untouched — every pipe, staircase and gap is still exactly where your muscle memory left it, and a proper run-up still clears the wide ones.

**The run also gets off your thumbs.** On the touch stick controls, the joystick and the JUMP button were sitting right on the ground line, swallowing the runner whole. The level now rides on a raised deck with a dark stage under it, so the controls live on the stage and the game lives above them. Desktop and split-screen co-op are unchanged.

**SUPER BUDDY CART loses its gray wall.** That looming concrete slab at the start of every regional track? The grandstand, projected the size of a mountain range because nobody ever told it how big a building is. It has been moved back from the grid, politely resized, and it now has the decency to disappear when you drive right past it. It still watches the racing. From a normal distance. Like a building.

**Walls keep their promises now.** Connected walls have always been supposed to share one health pool — 1.1× the sum of every segment, reinforcement included. The bookkeeping had leaks: reinforce a damaged run and the new layer only counted at the run's damage fraction; chew a lone wall down and then connect it, and it rejoined at health it no longer had; knock the end off a run and the survivors' shares got recounted wrong. All three are fixed and pinned down by an exact-math test suite: the pool now includes ALL of the reinforcement, all of the time, and a wall brings its real health to the run — no more, no less.
