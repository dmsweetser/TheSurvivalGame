# v1.29 — ON THE BUTTON

A small release with one job: a sword that answers the hand that holds it.

**THE MORDOR FUN RUN FIX:** Runners on the Shire road reported a haunted blade — the buddy's swing would fire on its own whenever the road turned north, no button pressed, as if the sword had opinions. It did not. A piece of shared steering logic, built long ago so the platformer's jump could be held higher, had quietly taught every road north of the Shire to double as a trigger. Tank crews reported the same ghost years ago and their road was walled off; the Mordor road, being newer, had not been.

The wall is built differently now. Holding a direction is steering, everywhere, full stop — and the one place where holding up means something more (a jump, held higher) keeps its rule on its own road only. On the Mordor road the swing belongs to the button again: the fire key, the pad's fire button, the FIRE zone on a phone. Same swing, same pace, same damage — it simply waits to be asked. Walk straight into a goblin empty-handed and nothing happens but the goblin. Press the button, and something happens to the goblin.

The road itself is untouched: a thousand moose, markers banked in gold, and your run still waiting exactly where you left it.

**GIFTS ON THE ROAD:** The road now provides. Fifty powerups stand along the way, one every twenty moose or so, bobbing over the dirt and waiting to be run through: white boxes with red crosses that mend fifty health on the spot (the very first gift on the road is always one of these, early, before the road gets mean), orange swords that double your swing's damage for thirty seconds, and cyan lightning that puts forty percent more road under your feet for twenty. The HUD keeps the seconds where you can see them, Tank-style. They are gifts, not savings: leave the cabinet or take a fall and whatever you were carrying is gone, and the fall puts the current stretch's gifts back on the road, same as its foes.

**SAVE YOUR SKIRMISH:** WAR! commanders: your skirmish is no longer a mayfly. A skirmish in progress now parks in its own slot — beside your campaign battle, never instead of it — every time the game saves, autosaves included. Walk away from the cabinet, close the game entirely, come back tomorrow: the field, your units, your buildings, your bits, the hives and their purses, the very clock, all exactly where the fight left them. Entering WAR! with a skirmish saved raises the panel: RESUME SKIRMISH, or NEW SKIRMISH — and that second choice asks twice before it throws a battle away. The pause menu's third row saves a skirmish just like a campaign now. Campaign commanders keep everything they had; the two saves never touch.
