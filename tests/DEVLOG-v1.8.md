# THE SURVIVAL GAME — Devlog
## v1.8 — THE BUDDIES TAKE THE WHEEL (November 14, 2026)

The karts have drivers now, and the drivers are the Banzai Buddies. All of them. Yes, including the sink.

**SUPER BUDDY CART:**

- **Your kart is driven by YOUR buddy** — species, coat and name straight off your own Banzai Buddy, glasses firmly on. The moose's antlers stick up over the seat; the platypus drags its tail; the kitchen sink brought its faucet. Your buddy is sitting in the waiting-room kart too, ready when you are.
- **The rival seats are fellow buddies from the stable** — BELEG, ELROND, LEGOLAS and friends, one of each species, with their names floating overhead when you close in. Overtaking a stranger was fine; overtaking ELROND is personal.
- **The circuit got dressed**: trees and tire stacks all the way around, a grandstand full of extremely small fans on the top straight, a start gantry with a banner, and painted centre dashes rushing underneath you.
- **The racing got touched up**: a proper launch off the line, AI rivals that take the racing line and mind the traffic, gentler kart-to-kart bumps — and a DRIFT. Hold a hard steer at speed and the rear steps out; let go and the stored grip fires you forward.
- The Mode 7 view and the head-to-head WAITING ROOM from 1.7 ride along, of course: two players, two sides of the screen, kart colours picked, nobody dragged in.

**TANK RESCUE:**

- **The enemy has been told to calm down**: slower fire, softer shells, a player cannon that bites harder, and wrenches that patch +40.
- **GRENADES.** The WALL button (F, or pad X) swaps between the cannon and the grenade pouch. Lobbed, area blast, clears cover, settles arguments. Mind the splash up close. Deliveries and wrench pickups restock the pouch.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
