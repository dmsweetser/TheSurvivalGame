# v1.17 — SUPPLY LINES

WAR! grew a quartermaster. Battles can take a breath now, the bits flow richer, and your army finally knows how to walk around a wall.

**PAUSE, SAVE, COME BACK.** The X in the corner no longer abandons the fight. It opens a pause menu: RESUME, RESTART BATTLE, or SAVE & QUIT — and a saved battle is saved *properly*, folded into your game save with every unit, building, queue, rally flag and lifted patch of fog intact. Quit mid-battle, close the game, come back tomorrow: the war is exactly where you left it. It even rides along in the ordinary autosave, so a battle in progress is never lost.

**A RICHER FIELD.** You drop in with 250 bits instead of 150, the code fields grow from seven nodes to nine (4,800 bits waiting in the ground, up from 2,900), and harvesters haul 25 at a time. And the battlefield itself pays: order your troops to smash the trees, rocks, spools and wrecked terminals littering the field and every piece of debris drops salvage bits. Clear-cutting is now a legitimate economic policy.

**EVERYBODY PATHFINDS.** No more harvesters pinned behind a firewall, nose to the barrier, dreaming of the other side. Units that find their way blocked now think in routes — around the wall, through the gap, across the field. Bugs too, worse luck.

**GATES.** The DEFENSE shelf gains the Gate: cheap, gold-posted, and polite. Your units walk straight through it; bugs have to stop and chew. Line your firewalls with gates and your harvesters commute while the swarm queues up outside like it needs a ticket.

**QUEUES AND RALLY POINTS.** Training stacks nine deep per building — pay up front, tap MELEE three times, and get back to the fighting while the Patch Bay cooks. Then tell the building where its work goes: select a Core, Patch Bay or Assembly Plant, tap anywhere on the field, and every unit it produces marches straight to that flag.

**A TIDIER WAR TABLE.** The command bar is sorted into TRAIN, BUILD and DEFENSE — tap a shelf and its wares spread out above the bar. Fewer buttons shouting at once; the same war.

**AND ONE SMALL MERCY IN THE RUN.** Super Buddy Run eases off the throttle a touch more — and the piranha bugs in the pipes, which on touch and split-screen were drawn *inside* the pipe while their teeth worked invisibly above it, now rise where you can actually see them. Fair is fair.

SQUASH BUGS. WEAR THE EVIDENCE.
